Recalled products, refunded through PayPal

An AI agent watches the US government recall feed, finds a store’s affected buyers in its PayPal orders, and lets each buyer settle in one tap.

Creates a demo store with real recalls and sandbox PayPal orders. About 20 seconds.

About 6% of buyers act on a recall announced only by press release; about 50% act when notified directly.(CPSC data, FY2013–16, as cited by consumer groups)

How it works

AI suggests, you approve, fixed rules move the money.

  1. 1MODEL

    The agent finds a matching recallAI

  2. 2YOU

    You approve the campaignYou

  3. 3TOOL

    Buyers tap their link and sign in with PayPalBuyer

  4. 4RULE

    PayPal refunds, pays out, or tracks a replacementRules decide which

What PayPal does here

All calls run in the PayPal sandbox.

Order records

Finds who bought the recalled item

Log In with PayPal

Checks the buyer owns the order

Refunds

Orders paid within 180 days

Payouts

Orders paid more than 180 days ago

Shipment tracking

Adds replacement tracking to the order

Webhooks

Updates each case when PayPal reports back
