What PayPal does here

All calls run in the PayPal sandbox.

  • Order recordsFinds who bought the recalled item.
  • Log in with PayPalChecks the buyer owns the order.
  • RefundsOrders paid within 180 days.
  • PayoutsOrders paid more than 180 days ago.
  • Shipment trackingAdds replacement tracking to the order.
  • WebhooksUpdates each case when PayPal reports back.
